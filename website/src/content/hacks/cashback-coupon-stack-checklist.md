## Separate immediate savings from future value

An instant coupon lowers today’s checkout total. Cashback, points, and store rewards usually arrive later and may expire or require another purchase.

## Check eligibility independently

Read the terms for each layer. Confirm the product, seller, payment method, purchase channel, minimum spend, exclusions, and expiration date.

## Use one clean calculation

```text
Shelf price:
Instant retailer discount:
Manufacturer coupon:
Checkout subtotal:
Card-linked offer:
Portal cashback:
Future store reward:
Estimated tax and shipping:
Immediate out-of-pocket cost:
Net effective cost after confirmed rewards:
```

Do not subtract a percentage from a price that was already reduced by the same promotion. Keep pending rewards labeled as pending until they post.

## Save evidence

Keep screenshots of activated offers, checkout, order confirmation, and the final reward posting. This makes missing-credit claims much easier.
