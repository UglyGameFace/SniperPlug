## Lock the location first

Set the correct ZIP code and store before trusting a price or stock count. Recheck the selected location after opening a shared link because retailer pages can silently switch stores.

## Match the item identifier

Use the UPC, SKU, model number, or offer ID whenever available. Similar product names can hide different sizes, generations, bundles, or conditions.

## Treat inventory as a lead—not a promise

Local inventory can lag behind sales, returns, damaged units, and misplaced stock. Call the store only when the potential savings justify the time, and ask staff to verify the exact identifier.

## Verify the live price

Use the retailer app, an official scanner, or checkout. A colored tag, old screenshot, or third-party count is not final proof.

## Record the result
