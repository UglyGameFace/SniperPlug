## Read the condition definition

Retailers use labels such as excellent, satisfactory, certified refurbished, or used-like-new differently. Read the exact definition attached to the listing.

## Verify what is included

Compare the box contents against the manufacturer’s list. Check chargers, remotes, stands, adapters, manuals, mounting hardware, and proprietary cables.

## Inspect and test immediately

Photograph the packaging and item before setup. Test power, ports, buttons, display, battery, connectivity, serial number, account locks, and factory reset status.

## Confirm protection

Record the return deadline, restocking fee, warranty source, and whether the manufacturer recognizes the serial number. A deep discount is not worth losing a workable return path.
