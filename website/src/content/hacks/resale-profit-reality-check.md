## Use sold data, not asking prices

Active listings show what sellers hope to receive. Recent sold listings show what buyers actually paid. Match the exact model, size, color, condition, and included accessories.

## Calculate the conservative margin

```text
Purchase total with tax:
Expected sale price:
Marketplace fees:
Payment fees:
Shipping and packaging:
Return / damage reserve:
Estimated net profit:
Expected time to sell:
```

Use the lower end of recent sold prices and a realistic shipping estimate. A deal is not a flip merely because the sticker discount is large.

## Check exit options

Know the retailer return deadline and the likely local-sale value. Avoid tying up money in slow inventory unless the margin compensates for the wait and risk.
