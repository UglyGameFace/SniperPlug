## Match the exact product

Confirm the model number, size, color, pack count, condition, and included accessories. A discounted option is not useful when it is a different variant from the one shown in the headline.

## Confirm the seller and fulfillment

Check who sells the item and who ships it. Marketplace sellers can have different prices, return rules, warranties, and delivery estimates than the retailer itself.

## Rebuild the final price

```text
Item price:
Required membership:
Coupon or promo:
Cashback or store credit:
Shipping:
Tax:
Final checkout total:
```

Treat a clipped coupon or future reward as separate from the immediate checkout price. Never count the same discount twice.

## Capture proof

Save the product URL, exact variant, seller, displayed price, checkout total, and verification time. Prices and inventory can change quickly.

## Buy-or-pass rule

Buy only when the exact item is correct, the final cost is verified, the return policy is acceptable, and the deal still makes sense without manufactured urgency.
